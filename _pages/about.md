Hi, I'm {{ site.author.name }}, a Computer Science and Engineering graduate of IIT (BHU), Varanasi, currently working as a software engineer at Oracle in Bengaluru.

I'm driven by a desire to understand how intelligent systems learn, make decisions, and cooperate. I enjoy programming and mathematics as ways to investigate a question: formulate an idea, build a model, examine its assumptions, and learn from the results. I want to understand why a method works, where it breaks down, and what those limitations can teach us.

My interests span machine learning, reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and large language models (LLMs). I'm particularly curious about how reward design shapes cooperation, how AI agents combine information with tools, and how we can evaluate their behavior beyond a few successful examples. My academic projects in cooperative multi-agent learning and residential energy forecasting have given me opportunities to explore the relationship between modeling choices, data, and observed behavior.

I'm enthusiastic about learning new technologies and deepening the theoretical foundations behind them. I value careful experimentation, clear mathematical reasoning, and reproducible evidence. I hope to develop these interests through sustained study, open-ended research, and conversations with people who challenge my assumptions and introduce me to questions I have not yet considered.

Outside engineering, I enjoy mentoring, boxing, and running. I also value sharing what I've learned and learning from others.
