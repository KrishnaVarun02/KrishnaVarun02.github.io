Hi, I'm {{ site.author.name }}, a software engineer at Oracle in Bengaluru and a Computer Science and Engineering graduate of IIT (BHU), Varanasi. My work spans AI-assisted engineering, cloud and blockchain infrastructure, and real-time data systems.

I enjoy programming, solving problems, and turning ideas into working software. I'm interested in how systems work beneath the surface, from algorithms and data structures to the services and data pipelines that connect an application. Building things gives me a way to connect these fundamentals with practical problems.

Artificial intelligence is a particular area of interest for me, especially large language models (LLMs), AI agents, and multi-agent systems. I'm curious about how models can use tools, retrieve relevant information, and coordinate to handle multi-step tasks. My interests also extend to machine learning and reinforcement learning, and to the engineering questions involved in making AI systems useful, reliable, and easier to evaluate.

I'm enthusiastic about learning new technologies and broadening my understanding of software engineering. I enjoy exploring unfamiliar tools and frameworks, experimenting with different approaches, and understanding the tradeoffs behind technical decisions. Whether I'm working with AI, cloud infrastructure, or data systems, I want to keep learning and become a more thoughtful engineer.

Outside engineering, I enjoy mentoring, boxing, and running. I also value sharing what I've learned and learning from others.
