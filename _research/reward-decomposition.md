This research project explores cooperative Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) using LOMAQ under centralized training with decentralized execution (CTDE). It focuses on how decomposable reward functions can address structural credit assignment across collaborating agents.

## Approach

- Developed a MARL framework using LOMAQ within the CTDE paradigm.
- Implemented decomposable reward functions for credit assignment in cooperative multi-agent environments.
- Applied monotonic value decomposition constraints to align decentralized policies with global objectives.

## Evaluation

Evaluated convergence stability and reward optimization under varying agent-coordination settings.
